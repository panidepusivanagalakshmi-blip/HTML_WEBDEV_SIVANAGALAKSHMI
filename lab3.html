Tirumala-The Divine Abode on the Seven Hills” ✨🛕

Tirumala is a town in the Chittoor district of Andhra Pradesh, India. It is located on the seven hills of the Seshachalam range and is known for its famous Venkateswara Temple, which is one of the most visited pilgrimage sites in the world. The temple is dedicated to Lord Venkateswara, an incarnation of Lord Vishnu, and attracts millions of devotees every year.

Places to visit in Tirumala



  1. 🛕 Sri Venkateswara Swamy Temple (Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams)


  2. 🛕 Sri Kapileswara Swamy Temple , Tirupati


  3. 👣 Shrivari Paadaalu


  4. 💧 Akasa Ganga


  5. 🪨 Shilathoranam (Natural Arch) - Geo Heritage Site
