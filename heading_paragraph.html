CID

CID is an Indian crime detective television series that has been on air since 1998. The show follows a team of detectives from the Crime Investigation Department (CID) as they solve various criminal cases. The series is known for its suspenseful storytelling, intriguing plot twists, and memorable characters. It has gained a massive following over the years and has become one of the longest-running television shows in India.

roadies

MTV Roadies is an Indian reality television show that first premiered in 2003. The show features a group of contestants who travel to different locations and participate in various physical and mental challenges. The contestants are judged by a panel of celebrity judges, and the winner is determined by public voting. MTV Roadies has gained a massive following over the years and has become one of the most popular reality shows in India.
