HINDU GODS

The Hindu pantheon is vast and diverse, encompassing a wide range of deities, each with their own unique attributes, stories, and symbolism. Some of the most widely worshipped Hindu gods include:

1. Brahma: The creator god, responsible for the creation of the universe and all living beings. He is often depicted with four heads, representing the four Vedas (sacred texts) and the four directions.
2. Vishnu: The preserver god, who maintains the balance of the universe and protects it from evil forces. Vishnu is often depicted with blue skin and four arms, holding a conch shell, discus, mace, and lotus flow
er. He is also known for his ten avatars, including Rama and Krishna. 3. Shiva: The destroyer god, who is responsible for the destruction and transformation of the universe. Shiva is often depicted with a third eye, a crescent moon on his head, and a trident. He is also associated with meditation, yoga, and the arts.
4. Ganesha: The elephant-headed god of wisdom, knowledge, and new beginnings. He is often worshipped at the start of new ventures and is known for his ability to remove obstacles. Ganesha is depicted with a large belly, four arms, and a mouse as his vehicle.
5. Lakshmi: The goddess of wealth, prosperity, and fortune. She is often depicted with four arms, sitting or standing on a lotus flower, and holding gold coins and lotus buds. Lakshmi is worshipped during the festival of Diwali and is believed to bring good luck and abundance.
6. Saraswati: The goddess of knowledge, music, and the arts. She is often depicted with four arms, holding a veena (a musical instrument), a book, and a rosary. Saraswati is worshipped by students, artists, and musicians seeking wisdom and inspiration.
7. Hanuman: The monkey god, known for his strength, devotion, and loyalty. He is a central figure in the epic Ramayana and is often depicted with a mace and a mountain in his hands. Hanuman is worshipped for courage, protection, and success in endeavors.
